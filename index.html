FAQ

Questions?

Where do I access the courses?

Once you join, you get a login to the AIPE Labs LMS. All four 15-day courses are unlocked there — lessons, build tasks and starter repos — and your progress is tracked as you go.

What skill level do I need?

For the Claude Code, Codex and Scalable Engineering tracks, you should be comfortable with basic programming. Marketing for Founders needs no coding at all.

How much time do I need?

Plan for 1.5–2 hours a day for 15 days. The four courses are self-paced, so you can take the other tracks after the cohort.

Can I really ship something in 15 days?

Yes — if it's small. The cohort is scoped to one concrete outcome: a deployed app, a shipped feature, a live landing page, or a production-ready architecture. Small and shipped beats big and unfinished.

Is there a refund policy?

Full refund any time before the cohort starts. Once it begins, we're committed to the 15 days together.

Will the price go up?

Yes. ₹4,999 is the founding price for the first cohort. It exists to fill the room with serious builders and get real case studies. Later cohorts will cost more.
