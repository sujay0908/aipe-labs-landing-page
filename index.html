FAQ

Questions?

What skill level do I need?

You should know basic programming (any language). We'll teach the AI and product engineering parts. If you can write a function and use an API, you're ready.

How much time do I need per week?

Plan for 8–12 hours per week. That's roughly 1–2 hours on the module, 1.5 hours for each live session, and the rest on building your product.

What if I can't finish in 42 days?

You keep lifetime access to the alumni Slack and all pre-recorded content. The 42-day structure is designed to get you to a shipped MVP, not a finished product — ship something small, then keep building.

Is there a refund policy?

Full refund within the first 7 days if the cohort hasn't started. Once the cohort begins, we're committed to your 42-day journey together.

Why is it so cheap?

This is Cohort 1. The founding price exists to fill the room with serious builders and get real case studies. The price goes up to ₹7,999+ from Cohort 2.
