FAQ

Questions?

Are the courses really free?

Yes. All four 7-day courses are free on our Skool community — lessons, build tasks and starter repos. You only pay if you want the live sprint: live sessions, Slack support, a review of your work and a deadline.

What skill level do I need?

For the Claude Code, Codex and Scalable Engineering tracks, you should be comfortable with basic programming. Marketing for Founders needs no coding at all.

How much time do I need?

Plan for 1.5–2 hours a day for 7 days. The free courses are self-paced, so you can go slower.

Can I really ship something in 7 days?

Yes — if it's small. Each sprint is scoped to one concrete outcome: a deployed app, a shipped feature, a live landing page, or a production-ready architecture. Small and shipped beats big and unfinished.

Is there a refund policy?

Full refund any time before your sprint starts. Once it begins, we're committed to the 7 days together.

Will the price go up?

Yes. ₹2,999 is the founding price for the first cohort. It exists to fill the room with serious builders and get real case studies. Later cohorts will cost more.
